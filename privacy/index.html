Privacy Policy

Snip35 is engineered as a 100% on-device paper & print collage studio. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or train on your photographs or personal data.

1. Your Photos Never Leave Your Device

All photograph analysis, subject detection (Apple Vision & on-device segmentation), color matching, Metal GPU collage rendering, high-resolution print generation, and 60fps MP4 reel exports run 100% locally on your iPhone. Snip35 never uploads your photographs, magazine scans, or finished collages to any remote server.

2. Data Not Collected

Consistent with our Apple App Store Privacy Nutrition Label, Snip35 collects no personal data:

  • No account registration, login, email, or phone number is required to use Snip35.
  • No third-party advertising SDKs, tracking pixels, behavioral analytics, or data brokers are included in the app.
  • No cross-app or cross-site tracking is performed.

3. Device Permissions

Snip35 requests device permissions only when you initiate a specific creative action:

  • Photo Library (Selection): Handled via Apple's system Photo Picker (PhotosPicker), granting access only to the individual photo(s) you explicitly select.
  • Photo Library (Add-Only Save): Used solely when you tap Save to write an exported print, transparent PNG cutout, gallery mat, or editorial reel to your Photos library.
  • Camera: Used solely when you choose to take a photo or photograph a physical magazine spread for your collage.

4. Local Storage on Your Device

Saved collages, custom scanned pages, and app preferences (such as Ceramic/Obsidian appearance, haptic feedback, and studio sound toggles) are stored locally inside Snip35's private app container on your device. You may delete individual collages or scans at any time inside the app, or remove all app data by deleting Snip35 from your device.

5. In-App Purchases & Subscriptions

Snip35 Pro (in-app Studio) subscriptions and lifetime unlocks are processed securely by Apple via StoreKit. Apple handles all billing, receipts, refunds, and Family Sharing verification. Snip35 never receives or stores your credit card number, billing address, or Apple Account credentials.

6. Support Communications

If you voluntarily email us at support@snip35.com, we use your email address and message contents solely to respond to your support request and do not share them with third parties.

7. Children's Privacy

Because Snip35 does not collect personal information from any user, it does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

8. Changes & Contact

If we ever update this Privacy Policy, we will post the revised version at https://snip35.com/privacy/ with an updated effective date. Questions? Contact us anytime at support@snip35.com.
