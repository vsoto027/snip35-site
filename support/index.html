Support & FAQ

Quick answers for common questions—or reach us directly anytime and we'll get back to you quickly.

How do I restore my Snip35 Pro purchase on a new iPhone?

Open Snip35 → Settings (gear icon in the top right) and tap Restore purchases, or tap Restore purchase at the bottom of the upgrade screen. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple Account (or Apple Family Sharing group) used for the original purchase.

How do I cancel or manage my Annual subscription?

Subscriptions are managed directly by Apple. Open your iPhone Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions → Snip35 to change plans or cancel anytime at least 24 hours before renewal. You'll keep full Pro access through the end of your current period.

Why aren't studio sounds playing?

Snip35's tactile paper and scissor sounds obey your iPhone's hardware Silent switch and mix cleanly alongside your music. Flip off Silent mode (or turn up volume), and check Snip35 → Settings → Craft & motion to make sure Paper & scissor sounds and Tactile scissor haptics are enabled.

How do I scan my own physical magazine pages?

Tap the Sheets button in the top bar, then tap Add Page to photograph a physical magazine spread, vintage receipt, or book page—or import an image from Photos. Your custom pages live privately on your device.

What export formats does Snip35 support?

Tap Export or Reel on any finished collage to save 2.8K & 4.8K (300 DPI) prints, museum gallery mats, transparent scissor-cut PNG stickers & subject cutouts, or 5s / 10s 9:16 60fps MP4 editorial reels directly to Photos.

Does Snip35 upload my photos to the cloud?

Never. Every collage, cutout, and reel is computed 100% locally on your iPhone using Metal and on-device segmentation. Read our full Privacy Policy for details.

Still need a hand?

Send us a note at support@snip35.com with your iPhone model and iOS version so we can help right away. For billing refunds, Apple handles requests directly at reportaproblem.apple.com.
